WSUDOKU · ACCOUNT & DATA

Delete your account.

This page is the public deletion-request resource for WSudoku by Kvass Games, Android package com.KvassGames.WSudoku.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

FASTEST METHOD

Delete inside WSudoku

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review the warning, then confirm.

This method uses your authenticated session. A successful deletion signs you out and clears the account-scoped profile and scores kept by WSudoku on that device.

NO APP ACCESS NEEDED

Request deletion by email

Send a request from any device. Kvass Games will reply with the ownership-verification steps before deleting the account.

Email deletion request Perezhoginviktor@proton.me

Include your display name, platform, approximate last-played date, and a clear request to delete the account. Never email a password, device identifier, purchase token, or full payment receipt.

WHAT IS DELETED

Account-associated WSudoku data

After ownership is verified, deletion removes the server-side WSudoku account and its associated game data, including:

  • Nakama account, authentication links, and active sessions
  • display name, avatar, and public profile presentation
  • medals, XP, level, statistics, and achievements
  • theme, background, and frame unlocks and selections
  • personal and public leaderboard records
  • saved solo runs, results, wallet, and user-owned storage
  • active private-battle room reservation associated with the account

Kvass Games aims to acknowledge email requests within 7 days and complete a verified request within 30 days. If more information is needed to establish ownership, Kvass Games will reply to explain the next step.

WHAT MAY REMAIN

Local settings and limited safeguards

Data stored only on your device
An outside-app request cannot reach local files. Clear WSudoku's app storage or uninstall it to remove local language, audio, display, privacy, and consent settings.
Store entitlement
A Remove Ads purchase entitlement may remain with Google Play so an eligible purchase can be restored. It is not used as a WSudoku public profile.
Recorded deletion identifier
Nakama may retain the deleted account's internal identifier only so the deletion can be applied again if an older backup is ever restored. It is not an active game account.
Support correspondence
The deletion email and verification record may be retained for up to 90 days after completion for request security and audit, then removed unless a longer period is legally required.

Data that another provider processes independently, such as Google Play transaction records, is governed by that provider's account and retention controls.
